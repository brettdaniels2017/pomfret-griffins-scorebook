      How to keep the book

      • Fill in the lineup, opponent, and whether Pomfret is home or away before the first pitch. The visitor bats in the top of the inning.
      • Ball, strike, and foul track the count. Ball four is a walk. Strike three is a strikeout. A foul at two strikes stays at two strikes.
      • Tap the result when the ball is put in play. Bases, outs, the score, and the player line all move together.
      • Stolen base, caught stealing, and wild pitch move runners and leave the at-bat open.
      • Turn on “Advance runners on outs” when a groundout or flyout moves every runner one base. Sacrifice is an out that is not an at-bat.
      • Sub keeps the player’s line and puts a new hitter in that spot. Undo takes back the last play.

      What the numbers mean

      • AB at-bats. Walks, hit by pitch, and sacrifices are not at-bats. Reaching on an error is an at-bat and not a hit.
      • H hits. AVG is hits divided by at-bats. The card also shows on-base and slugging.
      • RBI runs driven in on hits, walks, hit by pitch, and sacrifices. Stolen bases and errors do not add an RBI.
      • IP is outs divided by three. ERA is earned runs per nine innings. Runs on errors are unearned.
      • Keys: B ball, S strike, F foul, 1–4 hits, K strikeout, W walk, U undo.
